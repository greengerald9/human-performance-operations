Coach
Test Guide

Test 1 — Pure 505
1
Cones at 0m, 5m, 10m in a straight line.
2
Player sprints from 0m to 5m cone. Plants foot. Returns to 0m.
3
Stopwatch starts on first movement. Stops at 0m on return.
4
Left foot plant and right foot plant tested separately. 3 reps each. 45–60s rest between reps.
Test 2 — Reactive 505
1
Same setup. Player does not know the direction in advance.
2
As player reaches the 3m mark — coach calls "Left" or "Right" verbally.
3
Record time, direction called, and correct first attempt — Yes or No.
4
Mix directions across 3 reps. Do not call the same side every time.
